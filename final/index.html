About me...

After earning my associate degree in web development, I realized how naturally my interior design background fits into this new creative space. I've always been drawn to how visuals tell a story—whether it's through a thoughtfully designed room or a clean, responsive website.

Over the years, I've built up a solid mix of tools and skills that help me bring ideas to life, including:

  • Adobe Creative Cloud – especially Photoshop, Illustrator, and InDesign
  • Canva for quick, clean layouts and mood boards
  • Revit and AutoCAD from my interior design days
  • HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for building and styling websites
  • Bootstrap and SASS to create responsive, scalable designs
  • Figma for wireframing and UI/UX design

Now, I approach web design the same way I approached interiors: with intention, balance, and a focus on how people move through the space. I love combining structure with creativity—whether it’s crafting a visual brand in InDesign or coding a layout that feels effortless to use.
